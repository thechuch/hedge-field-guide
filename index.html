Field Guide

Water, power, and irrigation reference. Works offline once loaded.

SETTLED Main bank is 4P

LiTime answered in writing: 6P is not supported and not advised. Main bank = 4P (~8 kWh usable, in warranty). The other 2 batteries run the shed freezer. Terminal torque confirmed: 7 to 10 N·m.

RIGHT NOW Water before the pond drops

The pond has a clock on it. The solar system does not. Measure the tank bulkhead and the pond lift today, order the pump port fitting kit, run the pump off one battery, and lay the fill line on the ground. Open the punch list →

LATER Before you wire the bank

Measure first: the bus stud size (likely 3/8″/M10, not M8) sets your ring-lug size. Also confirm your Iota model.

Buy: 2 charger-lead fuses (70A + ~30A) + 6ga/10ga lugs, the 2/0 jumper, and a non-combustible barrier for under the bus. Cut lengths →

BLOCKERS Before you energize

Five fire blockers are open: pull the front-wall pink foam (it faces the terminals), verify the 80A breaker's DC interrupting rating, cement board the power board face, shield the house wall, set the Tycon to Lithium 28.8V with a meter check. Fire safety →
