# Project Hedge, Field Guide

This repo is the mobile field reference for an off-grid solar and water build near Ashland, Oregon.

## Read this first

**Read `PROJECT-BRIEF.md` before answering anything about the build.** It has the person, the site,
every settled decision, the as-built dimensions, the safety rules, and the open items.

## Rules for every reply

- **Never use em-dashes or en-dashes.** Use commas, colons, periods, or parentheses. For ranges
  write "2 to 3", not "2-3". This is a hard rule with no exceptions.
- Be direct and flag problems early. Do not rubber-stamp a plan.
- Say plainly when you are unsure instead of guessing confidently. A wrong confident answer about
  a fitting size or a clearance costs real money and rework here.
- He is often on a phone in the field with dirty hands, so lead with the answer, then the reasoning.

## What this repo is

A static, mobile-first PWA served at https://thechuch.github.io/hedge-field-guide/

Each page is standalone HTML sharing `assets/site.css`. Several drawings are generated by python
scripts that compute the geometry and the schedules from the same numbers, so the drawing and the
table can never disagree. If you change a dimension, change it in the generator, not the HTML.

Bump `CACHE` in `sw.js` whenever content changes, so installed phones fetch the new version.

## Things that are settled, do not reopen

- **Battery bank is 4P**, not 6P. LiTime confirmed in writing that this model is rated for a
  maximum of 4 in parallel.
- **The ATS is out.** Nothing needs source selection, and its 20 to 30 second transfer delay could
  not protect a computer anyway.
- **This system powers an office outlet, not the house.** Never tie it into house wiring.
- **The enclosures mount on the deck rail, not the tiny house.** That wall has had mold trouble and
  the house is on a trailer.
