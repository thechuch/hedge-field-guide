Work you can do now

  1. Finish the empty-box layout. Keep the batteries and all sources disconnected. Reserve the full equipment clearances. Mark proposed locations before drilling.
  2. Measure the PV route. Record all three string routes from their free roof leads to the combiner. The current estimate is 15 ft one way. Include roof travel, bends and service allowance.
  3. Check the delivered parts. Record the TP60, TPDIN, IOTA, inverter, DIHOOL, fan and thermostat labels. Record busbar stud sizes, cable markings and fuse-holder ratings.
  4. Resolve the battery base. Support each complete battery footprint on a stable, moisture-resistant base. Keep terminals covered during fitting. The shelf with front and rear overhang has not been approved.
  5. Prepare the protection review. Use the release checklist with the electrician. Close the permanent equipment-ground path, source/bond arrangement, branch load budget and DC fault-protection design.
Recorded progress

All four batteries were reported disconnected. The front purple foam and foil-faced end piece were removed. Both new cabinet fans are mounted on their plates. Thermostats and the TP60 were ordered. No roof descent cables have been run. These are user reports, not fresh measurements of the installation.

What changed from the old guide
  • The original controller in the photos is a 30 A TP30. The 60 A TP60 is a separate order. The 150 V TS60 was considered but was not selected.
  • PV strings combine in a protected combiner beside the lower cabinet. The old roof Y-branch and half-inch conduit instructions are withdrawn.
  • An isolated ground rod does not provide the required equipment fault path. The solar metalwork needs a permanent bond to the approved building protective-earth system.
  • The standard LiTime batteries do not have confirmed automatic cold-charge protection. Both chargers need the temperature interlock.
  • Fans do not waive equipment clearances. Old fixed fuse, torque, cable-cut and precharge instructions are withdrawn where their basis was not confirmed.
  • The 80 A inverter branch does not support the inverter's full 3000 W rating. A separate branch design is required.
  • The old fire-response, battery bench-test and meter-based neutral-bond instructions have been removed from field use.
