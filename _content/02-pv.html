Combiner arrangement

  1. Pair panels 1/2, 3/4 and 5/6 on the roof. One inter-panel series link joins each pair. Label the free ends A+/A−, B+/B− and C+/C− after polarity verification.
  2. Bring each string pair down together. There are six current-carrying conductors plus the required equipment-ground conductor.
  3. Each positive enters its own MNEPV15-150-1PNP breaker before joining the positive combiner bus.
  4. The three string negatives join the separate PV-negative bar. Equipment-ground conductors join the PE bar.
  5. The combined pair goes through the verified manual disconnect and final temperature-controlled PV interruption stage to the controller's PV terminals.
  6. The controller's BAT terminals connect to the battery bus through their own coordinated protection. The LOAD terminals stay unused in this plan.

The received MNPV3 has its positive combining bus/output lug, insulated negative bar and ground bar. The loose positive bus is marked 3-073-2C. Fit it with the matching breakers according to the manual; keep the live positive bus insulated from the metal backplate. The enclosure needs three individual string breakers for this array. Its standard cartridge-fuse arrangement holds only two fuse holders. Its terminals provide the transition from the individual string conductors to the combined feeder. MidNite combiner manual.

Keep individual string protection.

A single 60 A breaker after combining does not replace the three 15 A string breakers. The other parallel strings can feed a fault in one string.

External mounting

Mount the MNPV3 and DIHOOL upright on structural backing beside the lower white cabinet. Put the DIHOOL nearest the cabinet PV entry. Preserve lid access, connector access, cable bends and suitable sealed entries. Keep each positive/negative pair together. Reserve space for the charge-inhibit stage and the controller-entry SPD. Permanent equipment bonding stays effective with the PV disconnect open.

Controller setup and limits
  • Keep PV isolated. Connect the controller to the correctly protected battery circuit first so it can detect the system voltage.
  • Confirm 24 V system detection. Configure the final LiFePO4 profile while PV remains isolated.
  • Use the LiTime charge-voltage basis of 28.8 V ±0.4 V. Final target, duration, float and restart values need a recorded profile. Disable equalization and lead-acid temperature compensation.
  • Check measured battery voltage with a suitable meter. The display alone is not sufficient.
  • Connect PV only after the complete release checklist passes. For normal shutdown, isolate PV before removing the controller's battery connection.
  • Do not put the inverter on LOAD terminals. LOAD-off does not establish that battery charging has stopped.
  • The 1500 W array is close to the TP60's 1560 W limit at 24 V. There is no approved panel expansion on this controller.

These steps give the required order after release. They do not authorize an unfinished circuit to be powered. TP controller ratings and interface.

Sunlit panels and roof-side leads can remain live when the combiner breakers are open. Keep connectors protected and never separate PV connectors under load.
