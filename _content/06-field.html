Test sequence after the design is released

  1. Inspect while isolated. Check part numbers, cable routes, protection, polarity and the recorded terminal torque for each exact component. Keep PV and grid charging isolated. Battery posts remain live even when external cables are removed.
  2. Test controls separately. Use the final protected control test setup. Prove startup, shutdown, sensor faults and charger-stop commands before full charging is possible.
  3. Prepare the four batteries. Follow the matching LiTime procedure for initial charge, voltage matching and parallel connection. Do not connect a mismatched or previously tripped pack into a live bank.
  4. Power the MPPT from BAT with PV isolated. Confirm system voltage and the full charge profile. Follow the final startup/precharge instructions for each load; no generic resistor or two-second precharge rule is approved.
  5. Inspect and commission PV. Before initial PV energization, a qualified installer verifies string voltage, polarity, isolation and protection against the released design. Keep within the recorded cold Voc and input limits. Enable initial charging only under the approved test procedure.
  6. Test each charging source in turn. After those PV checks, confirm actual charge current stops on a temperature trip. Prove both-source operation, hard override, loss of control power, failed probe, TPDIN reboot and network loss.
  7. Test the inverter branch and office outlet. Verify its protection and GFCI operation with the final neutral/PE arrangement. Increase load only within the approved branch rating.
  8. Record a loaded baseline. Check battery current sharing, voltage drop and joint/case temperatures at a stated load and ambient temperature. A hot joint needs investigation after isolation. Do not tighten live connections.
  9. Test winter and grid-loss behavior. Confirm heat can run from the battery bank and that charging stays inhibited until all batteries meet the restart conditions. Use safe test inputs; do not deliberately freeze-charge a battery.
  10. Save the final record. Attach settings, circuit drawings, torque references, measurements and test results. Recheck after repair or a part change. Follow the component maintenance instructions.
Normal isolation and emergency limits

For a planned controller shutdown, isolate PV first, stop the IOTA grid source, turn loads off, then use the approved battery disconnect sequence. Reconnection needs the battery-connected controller configured before PV is restored. The final labels must show every source and disconnect.

For smoke, unusual heating, swelling, hissing or a fire: move people away and call emergency services. Use an external disconnect only if it is safe to reach. Do not open or move a failing battery. PV upstream of its disconnect can remain live. Tell responders that both grid AC and a LiFePO4 battery/PV system are present.

The plastic storage box is not a tested battery fire enclosure. A loose lid, cement board lining or a handheld extinguisher does not establish containment of a battery event.
