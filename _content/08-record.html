Keep a field copy

  1. Open this page while you have a connection. Wait for the top status to say Saved for offline use.
  2. On iPhone, use Share → Add to Home Screen. Open the saved guide once while online.
  3. Test it before leaving: turn on airplane mode, reopen the guide, then restore your connection.
  4. Use Print / save PDF for a second copy. Expandable details are included in print.
  5. Export field notes separately. Browser storage belongs to this device and browser.

The guide, diagrams, parts list and spares list are included in the offline copy. External manufacturer manuals and shopping pages need a connection unless you save them separately. An old offline phone cannot receive corrections until it reconnects. Check the revision at the top before work.

Evidence order

  • Use the delivered component's exact manufacturer instructions and label for installation.
  • Use this page for the current system arrangement and unresolved work.
  • Use linked primary sources to check ratings and conditions. A source for a different model or revision does not close a check.
  • Older PDFs, screenshots and cached pages can contain retired instructions. Their dimensioned layouts and electrical ratings are not released by this page.

Primary sources are linked beside the relevant claims. The working technical records are maintained with the project. The public guide omits credentials, network addresses, exact property location and private contacts. Field notes stay local.

Unresolved manufacturer information
  • LiTime: whether these serial numbers have built-in low-temperature charge protection, terminal torque, approved external heating and warranty position.
  • Tycon: TP60 temperature compensation in Lit mode, terminal torque and surge withstand. The TP manual documents no low-temperature charge cutoff.
  • UltraHeat: 24 V warmer wattage, surface temperature, built-in limit, thermostat setpoints and lead time.
  • Inverter manufacturer: exact model, orientation/clearances, precharge, remote interface, AC protection and neutral/earth topology.
  • IOTA: delivered IQ-LIFEPO 24 V profile, AC switching/inrush and surge protection. Module identity is confirmed.
  • DIHOOL: actual factory lead size, fitted/mating connector compatibility and complete installation acceptance. Delivered identity and top-input/bottom-output direction are confirmed.
  • MC-9b and DDR-30G-24: final coil interface and fan load/startup budgets, protective coordination and installed temperatures.
  • TOBSUN EA15-5V: full input range, isolation and final irrigation assignment.
  • Thermal-control and SPD makers: final interfaces, failure behavior, installation modes and coordinated protection.
