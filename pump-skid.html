WIDTH IS THE BINDING DIMENSION

The three 10 in housings, the spin-down, and the end fittings use 33 of the 36 in width, leaving under 2 in past the fittings at each end. Build the hutch 40 to 42 in wide if you can. Height is not the constraint: only 36.5 of 48 in gets used.

Numbers are the flow order and the assembly order at once. Water enters low, goes left into the spin-down, up and across the housings, then down the right and out through the floor. Scroll the drawing sideways on a phone.

Why the spin-down is vertical and on its own

Four housings side by side do not fit 36 in with wrench room. The spin-down solves that for free: its ports are on the head and the clear body is designed to hang down, so it mounts vertically on the riser between the pump and the filter row. That leaves the three 10 in housings across the top at 6.5 in centers, and it puts the flush valve down low where you can crack it into a bucket in ten seconds without shutting anything off.

Four things that are easy to get wrong

1. The drain tee goes UPSTREAM of the check valve

Downstream, the check valve holds the whole filter bank full of water all winter. The tee has to be the lowest point on the pressure side.

2. Leave 5 in of clear space under the sumps

You need it to drop each 10 in sump straight down for a cartridge change. Nothing mounts in that band. It is drawn as a real dimension for a reason.

3. The 51S01 is hand tighten, suction side only

Its own label says so. A wrench cracks the housing, and it is not rated for the pressure side.

4. Mount the pump LOW

The SEAFLO self-primes about 6 ft. Every inch higher is prime margin you lose in August when the pond is down. Low on the board, not at a comfortable working height.

Electrical

24V DC from the deck box on the POND 7.5A blade fuse, riding the water trench. The ERSIQI float switch in the tank cannot switch this pump: it is rated 1A switching and the pump draws about 3.5A. The float switch drives a relay coil and the relay contacts switch the pump. That relay is a separate device from the Baomain contactor, and it is still on the buy list.

Winterizing this skid

1. Open the drain ball valve (10) into a bucket.
2. Pull the cap off the priming tee (3) and blow low-pressure air in, pushing the filter bank out the drain.
3. Break the union at (2), pull the foot valve and suction line out of the pond, let it drain downhill. A foot valve holds water in the suction line, so it will not drain until you disconnect it.
4. Unbolt the skid, 4 lags, and carry it inside.
5. Indoors: both check valves, the cartridges and bowls, the foot valve, and any gauge. A diaphragm pump does not clear itself with a dry run.
