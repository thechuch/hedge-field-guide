Ask in the Field

Two ways to ask questions with photos while you are out working.

The trick: point it at this site

A fresh assistant on your phone does not know this project. But the whole build is written down at a public URL, so you can just tell it to go read that first. Copy the prompt below, paste it, attach your photo, and ask.

I'm working on my off-grid solar and water build. Before answering, please read these two things so you have the full context: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/thechuch/hedge-field-guide/main/PROJECT-BRIEF.md https://thechuch.github.io/hedge-field-guide/ Two rules: never use em-dashes or en-dashes in your replies, and tell me plainly if you are unsure rather than guessing. My question:

Paste it, type your question at the end, attach a photo if it helps.

Two ways to do it

Claude app or claude.ai in your phone browser

Easiest. Paste the prompt above, attach a photo of whatever you are looking at, ask. It reads the brief and the guide, so it knows your battery bank is 4P, which way the inverter mounts, what the clearances are, and so on.

Best for: "which way does this go", "is this the right fitting", "what size should this be", quick sanity checks.

claude.ai/code in your phone browser

Claude Code also runs in a browser, so it works on your phone. Open the hedge-field-guide repo and it has the brief and every page in this guide as actual files, no pasting needed.

Best for: when you want it to actually update a page or a drawing while you are standing there.

What it will not know

Phone Claude gets the brief and the diagrams, not the full conversation. It will not remember the reasoning behind every decision, the things ruled out and why, or the exact photos you sent before. So it is sharp on facts and weaker on history.

Use it for: in-the-moment questions, checks, and measurements.
Come back to the desktop for: design changes, new drawings, or anything where being wrong is expensive.

Keep the brief current

The brief is only as good as its last update. When a real decision changes on the desktop, it gets written into PROJECT-BRIEF.md, and phone Claude picks it up automatically the next time you paste that prompt.
