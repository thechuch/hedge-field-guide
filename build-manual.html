    How to read this

    A STOP chip means do not go past that step until it is genuinely resolved. Some are fire blockers on the battery bank, some are decisions that change what you buy, and a few are rules that must never be broken: never wire the float switch straight to the pump, never tie the inverter into the house panel, never blow winterizing air in at the priming tee.

    Ticks live on this device only. If you check things off on your phone they will not appear on the laptop. Pick one and stay with it.
